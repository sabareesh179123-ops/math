Concept Warm-Up

Direct vs. Inverse Proportion

Direct Proportion ↑ ↑  or  ↓ ↓

Both Quantities Move Together

When quantity x increases, quantity y increases by the same multiplying factor. Their ratio stays constant!

x / y = k   (Constant)
x₁ / y₁ = x₂ / y₂
Example: More pencils bought → Higher total cost (at fixed price per pencil).
Inverse Proportion ↑ ↓  or  ↓ ↑

One Rises, The Other Falls

When quantity x increases by a factor, quantity y decreases by the same factor. Their product stays constant!

x × y = k   (Constant)
x₁ × y₁ = x₂ × y₂
Example: More taps filling the same tank → Fewer minutes needed.

Which Pairs Vary in Inverse Proportion?

(a) Tap to Reveal ↻

Number of identical taps filling a tank vs. time taken to fill it.

Think: If we double the taps, what happens to the time?
Inverse Proportion ↑ ↓

More identical taps pour water faster, reducing fill time proportionally.

(Taps) × (Time) = Constant
(b) Tap to Reveal ↻

Number of painters hired vs. days needed to paint a fixed wall.

Assuming all painters work at the same steady rate.
Inverse Proportion ↑ ↓

More painters working at the same rate finish the fixed wall in fewer days.

(Painters) × (Days) = Total Man-Days
(c) Tap to Reveal ↻

Distance a car travels vs. quantity of petrol in the tank.

Assuming constant fuel efficiency (mileage).
Direct Proportion ↑ ↑

More petrol lets the car travel farther in the exact same ratio (Not inverse!).

Distance / Petrol = Mileage (k)

Cost of Pencils — Unitary & Ratio Method

Question 2: If the cost of 24 pencils is ₹120, what is the cost of 20 pencils?

Visual Ratio Bar Comparison
24 Pencils (Full Pack) ₹120
20 Pencils (5/6 of Pack) ?
Step 1: Identify Relationship & Given Values

More pencils ⇒ Higher cost (Direct Proportion).  Given: Cost of 24 pencils = ₹120

Step 2: Unitary Method (Cost of 1 Pencil)

Cost of 1 pencil = ₹120 ÷ 24 = ₹5

Step 3: Final Answer (Cost of 20 Pencils)

Cost of 20 pencils = 20 × ₹5 = ₹100

Building Water Supply & Key Assumptions

Rooftop Tank Capacity = 120 Family-Days
  • Every family consumes water at the same steady daily rate.
  • All 30 families draw water uniformly throughout the period.
  • There is no refilling, overflow, or leakage in the tank.

Question 3: A rooftop water tank supplies 20 families for 6 days. If 10 more families move in, how many days will the water last? What assumptions are needed?

Step 1: Find New Total Number of Families

Initial families = 20  |  New total = 20 + 10 = 30 families

Step 2: Set Up Inverse Proportion Equation (x₁y₁ = x₂y₂)

More families ⇒ Water lasts fewer days.  20 × 6 = 30 × x  ⇒  120 = 30x

Step 3: Solve for x (Days of Supply)

x = 120 ÷ 30 = 4 days
